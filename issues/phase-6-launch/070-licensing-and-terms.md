# [Phase 6] #070: Licensing, Copyright & Terms of Service for Published Artworks

**Phase:** Phase 6: Launch  
**Labels:** `phase:6-launch`, `legal`, `licensing`, `terms`  
**Milestone:** Phase 6: Launch  

---

## 1. Background & Context
Creators need absolute clarity regarding ownership of their visual creations, copyright of published scripts, and permissions granted to embedders and the platform. We must establish transparent terms and open licensing options.

## 2. Objective
Draft and commit terms of service, acceptable use policy, and creative licensing framework in `docs/terms-and-licensing.md`.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Creator Ownership & Rights:
  - Creators retain 100% intellectual property ownership and copyright of their original scripts and compiled visual outputs.
- [ ] Platform License:
  - Grants the platform a non-exclusive license solely to host, compile, cache, and serve the Scene IR for embed purposes.
- [ ] Creative Commons / Open Source Options:
  - Supports publishing under standard creative licenses (e.g. CC-BY 4.0, CC0 / Public Domain, or All Rights Reserved).
- [ ] Acceptable Use Policy (AUP):
  - Prohibits deceptive phishing visuals, hate speech, malware distribution, or crypto-mining scripts.
  - Takedown and DMCA reporting procedure defined.

## 4. Definition of Done (DoD)
- [ ] `docs/terms-and-licensing.md` committed and linked in Studio footer and publish dialog.
- [ ] License selector integrated into publish workflow settings.
