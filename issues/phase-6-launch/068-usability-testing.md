# [Phase 6] #068: Usability Testing with 5 Non-Programmers (Time-to-Visual Validation)

**Phase:** Phase 6: Launch  
**Labels:** `phase:6-launch`, `ux`, `user-testing`, `metrics`  
**Milestone:** Phase 6: Launch  

---

## 1. Background & Context
The ultimate benchmark of `cfx` is its primary success criterion:
> **"A non-programmer makes their first visual in under 2 minutes."**
We must conduct qualitative and quantitative observational usability tests with actual target users (designers, digital artists, marketers, non-technical hobbyists) without guiding them.

## 2. Objective
Conduct 5 recorded usability testing sessions with non-programmers, measure time-to-visual, document points of friction, and synthesize actionable UX improvements.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Recruit 5 participants who do NOT have professional programming experience.
- [ ] Testing protocol:
  - Task 1: "Make a colored circle that grows and shrinks over time" (measure stopwatch time to visual).
  - Task 2: "Make it react to the mouse pointer".
  - Task 3: "Publish it and copy the embed code".
- [ ] Observation metrics:
  - Did they achieve a visual within 2 minutes? (Pass/Fail).
  - Where did they hesitate or misinterpret syntax?
  - Were diagnostic hints helpful when typos occurred?
- [ ] Usability findings report compiled and committed to `docs/usability-test-report.md`.
- [ ] Top friction points logged as follow-up refinement issues.

## 4. Definition of Done (DoD)
- [ ] 5 user sessions completed.
- [ ] `docs/usability-test-report.md` committed with metrics, quotes, and conclusions.
